Since 02/2025, as Consultant – Full Stack at Capgemini, Sohayb works on the mobile banking platform of a major Italian retail banking group. He delivers features end to end across all three layers: Java 17 / Spring Boot 3 microservices, an OpenAPI-first BFF on JDK 21 and the React Native / Redux Toolkit app. Typical work: designing and building new banking product flows and REST APIs from the OpenAPI contract through the BFF and microservice to the app state, including hand-offs to the bank's BPM processes. It is a large multi-repository programme released through several environments. The client is never named and individual features are not described in detail here.
